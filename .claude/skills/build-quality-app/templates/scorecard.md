# Scorecard (100 points)

| Category | Points | What a top score means (adapt to the app) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Educational / outcome value | 20 | The persona can do each promised outcome without the app and can name one thing they will do differently this week. |
| Content accuracy and quality | 15 | Facts, figures and terms are correct and current; clear at this persona's reading level; examples feel like their life. |
| UX and navigation | 15 | Every flow is obvious; no dead ends or "what now?" moments. |
| Functionality and reliability | 15 | Everything works; progress saves; reload and offline keep state; no console errors. |
| Engagement and interactivity | 10 | The persona wants to come back: habit plan, reminders, forgiving streak, feedback motivating not nagging. |
| Accessibility and inclusivity | 10 | Keyboard and screen reader work; contrast; reduced motion; examples include different ages, incomes and family types without judgement. |
| Visual design | 5 | Clean, consistent and calm on a phone, light and dark. |
| Performance | 5 | Fast first load and instant screen changes on a mid-range phone (state the throttle used and the numbers). |
| Privacy and security | 5 | Data stays on the device; no tracking; the persona would trust it. |

Bands: 90 to 100 outstanding, 80 to 89 excellent, 70 to 79 good, 60 to 69 satisfactory, below 60 needs improvement.

Rules: every point needs evidence; any total at 85 or above lists weaknesses; later-phase scope goes under Recommendations, not penalties; re-score from what you see in the latest round.
